# Asymptotic Econometrics with Python

Ali Habibnia

A course on asymptotic econometric theory, with Python examples. The outline connects probability limits to estimation and inference, including regression, likelihood, generalized method of moments (GMM), hypothesis testing, and resampling.

For graduate students, researchers, and quantitatively trained practitioners familiar with probability, calculus, linear algebra, and regression.

Use M01–M13 as the main study sequence. The advanced extensions cover specialised topics, including empirical processes, nonparametric inference, unit roots, and failures of regular asymptotics.

## Core sequence at a glance

| Module | Topic | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Convergence, stochastic orders, and rates | Modes of convergence, convergence rates, and stochastic remainder terms. |
| M02 | Laws of large numbers and sample moments | Conditions for consistent sample means and moments. |
| M03 | Central limit theorems and normal approximation | Scalar and vector limits, triangular arrays, and approximation accuracy. |
| M04 | Mapping results, studentization, and delta methods | Continuous mapping, Slutsky's theorem, and nonlinear transformations. |
| M05 | Dependence structures for time series | Stationarity, ergodicity, martingale differences, and mixing. |
| M06 | Dependent-data limit theorems and long-run variance | LLNs, CLTs, long-run variance, and HAC estimation. |
| M07 | Identification, uniform convergence, and consistency | Conditions establishing identification and estimator consistency. |
| M08 | OLS and dynamic regression | Consistency and asymptotic distributions under independence and dependence. |
| M09 | M-estimation, Z-estimation, and nonlinear least squares | Estimating equations and asymptotic linearization. |
| M10 | Likelihood, quasi-likelihood, and misspecification | Likelihood theory, misspecified models, and sandwich covariance. |
| M11 | Instrumental variables and GMM | Moment conditions, weighting matrices, efficiency, and overidentification. |
| M12 | Confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, and local power | Wald, score, and likelihood-ratio tests; local alternatives. |
| M13 | Bootstrap methods | Resampling for independent observations and time series. |

## Advanced extensions

| Module | Topic | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A01 | Weak convergence of stochastic processes | Tightness, Brownian motion, and functional central limit theorems. |
| A02 | Empirical processes and stochastic equicontinuity | Uniform laws, Glivenko–Cantelli classes, and Donsker theory. |
| A03 | Quantiles, nonsmooth statistics, and statistical functionals | Quantile representations, influence functions, and functional delta methods. |
| A04 | Nonparametric estimation and inference | Kernel and series methods, smoothing bias, and uniform inference. |
| A05 | Unit roots and near-unit-root asymptotics | Alternative normalizations and Brownian-functional limits for persistent time series. |
| A06 | Failures of regular asymptotics | Boundary parameters, weak identification, and heavy tails. |
